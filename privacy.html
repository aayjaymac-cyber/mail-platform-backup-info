Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Mail Platform Backup is a private tool used by its operator to back up and recover the operator's own Mail Platform installation.

Google Drive access

The tool uses rclone and Google OAuth authorization to access the operator's Google Drive. Drive permissions may allow access beyond the backup folder. The configured backup job uses that access to upload, list, verify, retrieve, and manage recovery archives.

Data stored

Recovery archives include application source, configuration, credentials, and database records, which may contain email addresses, campaign content, and sending and feedback history. Archives are encrypted locally through rclone crypt before upload and decrypted locally when retrieved.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only for backup and recovery operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Google Drive stores the encrypted archives under its own terms.

Access and deletion

The operator can revoke the tool's access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not delete existing archives; the operator can delete those separately. Archives remain until removed by the operator or configured retention rules.

Contact

For questions about this tool, contact its operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
